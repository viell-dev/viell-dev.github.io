# Game Review: Dimhaven - The Lost Source

> Not Recommended - 10.7 hours on record.  
> Originally posted as a [review on Steam][1], 26 June 2026.

Dimhaven fails to meet my expectations for a game meant to follow up Quern. I'm not even talking
about the few bugs I encountered; I can deal with those. The story was underwhelming. The pacing
was weirdly off: sometimes normal, sometimes hyperspeed, like when an NPC just appears behind you
even though there was no way for them to get there.

The puzzles are the main part of the game that I'm sure most players are interested in. Most of
them are medium to high difficulty, but you can figure them out if you look around enough and pay
close attention to things. Some puzzles are very hard. You're given just enough information to
solve them, but if you don't piece the parts together correctly, you can get stuck for quite some
time, trial-and-erroring your way through.

There is a hint button in the inventory screen, but I don't know what it does. I never pressed it.
I'm too stubborn for that. I'll bash my head against a puzzle until I solve it.

For one of the puzzles, you also had to find components in the surrounding environment, and one
managed to elude me for a very long time. I ran around the area many, many times before I happened
upon the correct configuration of things to find it. It made perfect sense afterward, but you can
easily become blind to things like that.

Overall, the puzzles are mostly good, though a bit too hard at times, and the story is subpar. The
atmosphere is great unless there are teleporting NPCs nearby, because that just screws with you.
But that might be intentional. Who knows.

**TL;DR:** I wouldn't recommend Dimhaven overall, but if all you care about is challenging puzzles,
it's decent.
