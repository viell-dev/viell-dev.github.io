# Game Review: D-topia

> Recommended - 8.1 hours on record.  
> Originally posted as a [review on Steam][1], 14 July 2026.

Got the "happy ending" achievement on my first run-through. I liked the game; the setting is
interesting, and the characters are likeable. Some of the decision-making sequences are a bit
confusing because there is no back button, but at least most paths have a bailout option at the
end. You can also just quit the game and continue from the autosave.

The puzzles are very easy. For someone not accustomed to logic puzzles, the later or optional ones
might take some trial and error, but overall, they serve better as a narrative tool for the lore
than as actual challenges.

I'm not sure whether I'll go back for the other endings or choices. I feel like I finished the game
in a good state. There are also some collectables if you're into that.

Overall, it's a chill story game. I'd rate it around 7/10.
