# Game Review: MIO: Memories in Orbit

> Recommended - 43.4 hours on record.  
> Originally posted as a [review on Steam][1], 31 January 2026.

Great medium sized metroidvania. Art style is lovely making exploration a joy. The endings are not
mutually exclusive so go wild, you won't lock yourself out of anything.

Besides some of the late game bosses most of them are pretty easy after learning their moves since
they telegraph their moves well in advance. The telegraph time lessens when you get into the late
game bosses and it can be a bit too fast at times even for a metroidvania fan like me. So some
bosses I cleared by dodging moves with luck instead of skill.

The mods (like Hollow Knight charms) are fun, there are a bunch of interesting combinations. I
found myself changing my builds between boss-fight builds and exploration builds if I didn't
oneshot an unexpected boss with the exploration build.

My favourite mods are Sharpened Hairpin for both movement and combat. Splintering Dodge, and The
Heart's Favourite for movement. And Energy Leecher and Foolish Ideal also work well with Sharpened
Hairpin and maybe Wild Cat if you have the energy for combat. Kinetic Thrust and The Hand's Greed
are also really good, especially the latter one.

To get more mod points use the Resource Shortage, Split Process and maybe Thinner Frame if you need
more mod points. Also Nacre Drought for boss fights, you shouldn't need to care about healing or
money in a boss fight, and one extra health is not worth it when you can deal more damage instead.
There's more than enough Nacre (money) to go around in the game. I finished 100% of the game with
almost 10k solid Nacre left and nothing to use it on.

Finally, let me touch on the health decreases. I personally didn't find it too bad, since the
bosses and enemies also get weaker and you find more health by exploring anyway. It balances out so
you still feel stronger. So it's not a big deal.

Overall 9/10 game, I had some issues with down-attacks not going through which made me rage a bit
at the harder platforming sections, but overall it was a great game.
