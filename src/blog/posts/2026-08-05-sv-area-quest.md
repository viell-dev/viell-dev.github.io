# Challenge Run: My Pokémon Scarlet and Violet Area Quest

I've been doing a challenge run in Pokémon Violet since March of this year (2026), on and off. I'm
calling it an "Area Quest" after the "Badge Quest", which I feel is spiritually similar. I've since
come to know of similar challenges that I link in the [Similar Challenges](#similar-challenges)
section. But for now I'm just going to explain my challenge and formally define its rules, as I see
them. This was designed and tested in Pokémon Violet, but should mostly translate to other
open-world Pokémon games as well, though it may need some tweaking.

The idea is that each **area** in the game is **locked** and you need to catch a shiny Pokémon to
progress to the next. So, what is an area? In Scarlet and Violet, this would be any location whose
name shows up on screen when you enter it **and** that has wild Pokémon the first time you can visit
it during the story. That rules out several locations, which will be considered free areas. Building
interiors, like shops, gyms, the academy, Zero Gate, and the Zero Lab, are not areas at all. They
are implicitly unlocked together with the area they belong to.

## Setup and Rules

### Areas

An area can be locked, available, or unlocked. It can also be a free area, which is unlocked when
you first visit it after starting the challenge. So setup doesn't count, and neither does accidental
entry. Passing through a locked or available area to reach another area is illegal. To unlock any
other area, you need to catch a shiny Pokémon there. Which shiny counts, and the optional
_Duplicates Clause_, are covered in [The Rules](#the-rules).

An area counts as connected if there is a natural path to it that you can access by just walking as
your character, not riding Koraidon or Miraidon, with the exception of **Swim** for the four
cardinal seas. So no going from _South Province (Area One)_ directly to _South Province (Area Four)_
or _South Province (Area Five)_; take the land bridges instead after unlocking _South Province (Area
Two)_ or _South Province (Area Three)_, respectively. That's how I played, and it feels most in the
spirit of the challenge to me, but it's not a hard requirement. If you have unlocked **Climb**, feel
free to use it. You do you.

_See the [Checklist](#checklist) section for details on which areas are free and how they connect._

### The Setup

Start a new game. That's it. Optionally, you can speedrun unlocking **Ride** on Koraidon or
Miraidon, then go back to _Cabo Poco_. Either way, the challenge begins at _Cabo Poco_ with every
area still locked, no matter what you passed through during setup. You can use your starter until
you catch your first shiny Pokémon at _Poco Path_, then it must be boxed.

### The Rules

You are only allowed to enter areas that are _available_ or _unlocked_. When you _unlock_ an area,
its connected areas (see above) become _available_, allowing you to hunt for shiny Pokémon in them.
Team Star bases are not real areas but sub-areas of the area their entrance is in, so you can't
enter a base until that area is unlocked. _Unlocking_ a base means clearing its Star Barrage and
obtaining the badge. Since bases don't connect to any areas, unlocking one doesn't make any areas
available. But where a base blocks the path between two areas, that path opens with the badge.

Story progress is only allowed in _unlocked_ areas. This includes Gyms, Star Barrages, and Titan
Pokémon. All of Area Zero, besides the Area Zero Underdepths, is one area. But before unlocking it
and allowing story progress, you are limited to the stretch between the start and close to the first
research base. Both the Great Crater of Paldea and the Area Zero Underdepths also have a few wild
encounters if you really want to include them, but skipping them is reasonable. So you can choose
whether you want to treat them as free areas.

The **first** shiny Pokémon you **see** in an area is the one you need to catch to unlock the area,
and you can only use Pokémon that unlocked an area in your party. The Pokémon must have the correct
location in its summary, so double-check if you were close to a border. Any other Pokémon are not
allowed to be used in your party. If you genuinely fail to catch it and can't reload a save to
retry, you can find another, just don't fail on purpose since that defeats part of the challenge.
Since these are all self-imposed rules, entering a locked area doesn't disqualify your run. If you
spot a shiny Pokémon in a locked area, you can go and catch it, treating it as briefly pausing the
challenge, but it doesn't count toward the challenge and the area stays locked.

**Duplicates Clause:** You can play with a duplicates clause to ignore Pokémon of the same
evolutionary line if you don't want duplicates. So Pichu, Pikachu, and Raichu are all duplicates to
each other under this clause.

### Kitakami

Kitakami needs a few exceptions to these rules. There you will find **segmented areas**: single
areas that the terrain splits into multiple parts, like _Oni Mountain_. The segments are listed
separately in the checklist to make pathing easier to follow, but they are still one area, so
unlocking any segment unlocks all of them at once.

Some areas have no walkable path at all. _Timeless Woods_ and _Fellhorn Gorge_ can only be entered
by falling in from a cliff above or using **Climb**, so for these areas that counts as the
connection. Similarly, the east half of _Mossfell Confluence_ requires **Swim** and **Climb**, and
_Kitakami Wilds_ (southeastern) requires **Climb**. The checklist notes these requirements on the
affected entries.

## Similar Challenges

The Badge Quest is the real inspiration for the Area Quest, with maybe a little bit of the Shiny
Professor Oak Challenge mixed in. Since starting my run I've come to know of other challenges in the
same spirit, so they deserve a shout-out too. As far as I can tell, none of these have a single
canonical origin. They grew out of the community, and forum threads are the closest thing to a
definitive source.

- **[Shiny Badge Quest][sbq]**: catch a shiny Pokémon before each Gym Badge. There are
  [PokéCommunity threads][sbq-old] going back years.
- **[Professor Oak Challenge][poc]**: catch every Pokémon available before each badge. The shiny
  variant does the same, but shiny.
- **[Shiny Route Challenge][src]**: catch a shiny on every route to progress, with a stricter [Shiny
  Challenge][sc] variant that includes every gift, static, and trade Pokémon.
